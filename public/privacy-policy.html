1 Introduction

Welcome to Founder for Founder (“we,” “our,” “us,” or the “Platform”), operated by Snalts.co.

Founder for Founder is a curated business network designed to connect founders, entrepreneurs, business owners, and professionals through meaningful relationships, business opportunities, networking events, referrals, and collaboration.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, submit a membership application, create an account, participate in community activities, attend events, or use our services.

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain it through an appropriate process.

2 Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information depending on how you use the Platform.

2.1 Personal and Contact Information

This may include:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile number.
  • WhatsApp number, where provided.
  • City or general business location.
  • Profile photograph.
  • Professional designation.
  • Business name and business contact details.

2.2 Business and Professional Information

To facilitate professional connections, we may collect:

  • Business name and category.
  • Business description.
  • Designation and professional experience.
  • Website and professional social media links.
  • Business services and expertise.
  • Business needs and offers.
  • Types of founders or professionals you wish to connect with.
  • Information submitted through leads, referrals, collaboration requests, or business opportunity listings.

Notice on Confidential Information: Please do not submit confidential business information, sensitive personal information, or another person's contact details unless you have the necessary authority and permission.

2.3 Membership and Application Information

When you express interest in joining Founder for Founder, we may collect:

  • Membership application details.
  • Information about your business and professional background.
  • Your reasons for applying.
  • Application status and related communications.
  • Email verification status.
  • Membership status, joining date, renewal date, and subscription records.

Submitting an application does not automatically guarantee membership or approval.

2.4 Payment and Transaction Information

If you purchase a membership or pay for an eligible service or event, payment-related information may include:

  • Transaction amount and currency.
  • Payment status.
  • Transaction or payment reference.
  • Subscription identifier.
  • Billing and invoice information.
  • Renewal and cancellation records.
  • Payment failure information.

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. We do not intend to store full payment card numbers, card security codes (CVVs), or raw payment credentials on our servers. Payment information is handled according to the applicable payment provider's terms and privacy policy.

2.5 Community Activity Information

When you use the Platform, we may process information relating to:

  • Member profiles and directory activity.
  • Connection requests and responses.
  • Needs & Wants posts and responses.
  • Leads and referrals.
  • Business opportunities and collaborations.
  • Event registrations, RSVPs, and attendance.
  • Challenge participation and submitted progress.
  • Member-submitted business outcomes and their verification status.
  • Messages, support requests, feedback, and reports.
  • Notifications and communication preferences.

2.6 Technical and Usage Information

Depending on the services enabled, we may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Login timestamps.
  • Pages visited and interactions with the Platform.
  • Security logs and error reports.
  • Cookie or similar technology identifiers, where used.

We use this information for security, troubleshooting, service improvement, and analytics where permitted by applicable law.

3 How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information for the following purposes:

  1. To receive, review, and manage membership applications.
  2. To verify email addresses and secure user accounts.
  3. To create and maintain member profiles.
  4. To operate the member directory and facilitate professional connections.
  5. To enable Needs & Wants, leads, referrals, and collaboration opportunities.
  6. To manage events, registrations, RSVPs, attendance, and meeting updates.
  7. To administer memberships, payments, renewals, and cancellations.
  8. To send account-related, transactional, security, and service notifications.
  9. To send event and community updates through email or WhatsApp where permitted and appropriately authorized.
  10. To moderate content, investigate reports, prevent fraud, and enforce our terms and community guidelines.
  11. To provide support and respond to enquiries.
  12. To maintain financial records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.
  13. To improve the reliability, functionality, and security of the Platform.
  14. To communicate changes to our policies, services, or membership arrangements.

We will not use personal information for purposes incompatible with those described in this Policy unless permitted by applicable law or supported by the necessary notice or consent.

4 Member Profiles and Information Visibility

Founder for Founder enables professional discovery and collaboration. Certain information may be visible to other members to facilitate these activities.

Depending on your settings and the features you use, this may include your name, profile photograph, business name, designation, business category, city, professional description, services, needs, offers, and relevant business links.

Private Contact Information Guarantee: Private contact information is not intended to be publicly visible by default. Email addresses and phone numbers should be protected through access controls and displayed only where authorized by your settings, an appropriate interaction, or another lawful basis.

Information you voluntarily publish in a community listing or opportunity post may be visible to authorized members. Avoid including confidential, financial, or sensitive personal information in public-facing posts.

We cannot guarantee that another member will not copy information they are legitimately able to view. Please exercise discretion when sharing information and report suspected misuse to us.

5 Email Verification and Account Security

We may use email-based one-time passwords (OTPs) to verify your email address and help secure your account.

Verification codes are intended for the relevant account action and may expire after a limited period. We apply appropriate safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, misuse, and repeated verification attempts.

Security Advisory: Never share your verification code with another person. Founder for Founder will not ask you to disclose your password or verification code through an unsolicited message.

6 Payments, Memberships, and Renewals

Membership payments and recurring subscriptions may be processed through third-party payment providers.

We may use transaction and subscription information to:

  • Confirm payment status.
  • Activate or renew membership access.
  • Maintain payment and invoice records.
  • Notify you of upcoming renewals or failed payments.
  • Process subscription changes or cancellations.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and resolve payment disputes.

Where recurring payments are enabled, the applicable price, taxes, renewal frequency, cancellation process, and other material conditions will be disclosed through the relevant membership or checkout process.

We do not store raw payment credentials on our servers. The payment provider may independently collect and process information under its own privacy policy.

7 Email, WhatsApp, and Other Communications

We may contact you through email, in-platform notifications, or WhatsApp where the relevant functionality is enabled and the required permissions or consent have been obtained.

Communications may include:

  • Email verification and account security messages.
  • Application updates.
  • Membership invitations.
  • Payment confirmations and renewal reminders.
  • Event announcements, invitations, and reminders.
  • Venue changes and schedule updates.
  • Community announcements.
  • Relevant service notifications.

Communication Protocol: WhatsApp is used for notifications and updates, not as the required platform for member networking. Members can access supported community features through the website.

You may opt out of optional promotional or non-essential communications where applicable. Essential account, security, payment, and service messages may still be sent when necessary and legally permitted.

You can manage WhatsApp preferences where available. Your communications with WhatsApp are additionally subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

8 Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties as a standalone data product.

We may share information in the following circumstances:

8.1 With Other Members

Information you choose to publish in your member profile, Needs & Wants posts, referrals, or other community features may be visible to authorized members according to the relevant feature and visibility settings.

8.2 With Service Providers

We may engage service providers to help operate the Platform, including providers for:

  • Website hosting and cloud infrastructure.
  • Database and file storage.
  • Email delivery.
  • Payment processing.
  • WhatsApp Business messaging.
  • Security monitoring.
  • Analytics and technical support.

These providers may process information on our behalf to provide their services, subject to applicable contractual, security, and legal requirements.

8.3 For Legal and Security Reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary and legally permitted to:

  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or lawful requests.
  • Protect the rights, safety, property, or security of Founder for Founder, its members, or others.
  • Investigate suspected fraud, abuse, security incidents, or violations of our terms.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

8.4 Business Transfers

If the business or Platform undergoes a merger, restructuring, acquisition, or transfer, relevant information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

We do not authorize third parties to use member information for unrelated purposes merely because they provide services to us.

9 Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Platform may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Maintain login sessions.
  • Support essential website functionality.
  • Improve security.
  • Remember preferences.
  • Understand website performance and usage, where enabled.

Where required, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect login or other website functionality.

The specific cookies and analytics tools used will depend on the Platform's actual technical configuration.

10 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including account administration, membership management, business operations, legal compliance, security, and dispute resolution.

Retention periods may vary depending on the information and its purpose. For example:

  • Account information may be retained while your account is active.
  • Membership and transaction records may be retained for applicable accounting, tax, legal, and dispute-resolution requirements.
  • Verification and security logs may be retained for a limited period appropriate to security needs.
  • Community posts may remain available while published or as otherwise permitted by the applicable feature and retention rules.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

Deleting an account may not immediately remove records that we are legally required or otherwise lawfully permitted to retain.

11 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, and misuse.

These measures may include:

  • Access controls and role-based permissions.
  • Secure authentication and email verification.
  • Encryption in transit where supported.
  • Secure password handling.
  • Protection of payment and integration credentials.
  • Monitoring and logging of relevant security events.
  • Backups and recovery procedures.
  • Restricted administrative access.
  • Security updates and testing.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and notifying us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

Where required by applicable law, we will address and report relevant personal data breaches through the prescribed process.

12 Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Manage communication preferences.
  • Update profile visibility and other available privacy settings.
  • Raise a complaint regarding the handling of your information.
  • Nominate another person to exercise applicable rights where the law provides for it.

To exercise a privacy right, email us using the address provided in Section 17. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

Withdrawing consent does not invalidate processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. Some information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations or address legitimate claims.

We will handle requests within the timelines and procedures required by applicable law.

13 Children's Privacy

Founder for Founder is intended for adults participating in professional and business networking.

The Platform is not designed to collect personal information from children. If we become aware that information has been collected from a child in circumstances where collection is not legally permitted, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

14 Third-Party Websites and Services

The Platform may link to third-party websites, social media platforms, payment providers, event venues, or external services.

We do not control the privacy practices, content, or security of these third parties. Your use of their services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

For example, Instagram content embedded or linked on our website may be subject to Instagram's policies and technologies.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing information directly to them.

15 International Data Processing

Some service providers may store or process information on infrastructure located outside your state or country.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take steps required by applicable law and use appropriate safeguards where necessary.

The locations and transfer arrangements will depend on the service providers and infrastructure used to securely deliver Platform services.

16 Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our legal obligations, technology, or community practices.

When changes are made, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy document. For material updates, we will provide additional notice through the website, email, or other appropriate community channels.

Your continued participation in the Founder for Founder community following any updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
