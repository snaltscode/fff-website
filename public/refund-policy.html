1 Introduction

This Refund and Cancellation Policy governs membership payments, subscription cancellations, and refund requests for Founder for Founder, operated by SnaLts.co (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

By applying for membership, accepting a membership invitation, or completing a payment, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

2 Application Verification and Voluntary Membership

Founder for Founder follows an application-based membership process. Applications are reviewed before membership is offered, and payment is requested only after the application has been verified and the applicant has been invited to proceed.

Membership is voluntary. Applicants are not required to purchase a membership simply because they submit an application or receive an invitation. Applicants may review the membership details, applicable fees, benefits, and terms before deciding whether to proceed with payment.

By completing the payment, the applicant confirms their decision to join voluntarily and acknowledges the membership terms presented at the time of payment.

3 General Refund Policy

Membership fees are generally non-refundable once successfully paid.

This is because membership is offered following an application verification process, and payment is collected only when the applicant has chosen to proceed with membership.

Non-Refundable Circumstances: Refunds will not ordinarily be provided for reasons such as:

  • A change of mind after completing the payment.
  • A decision not to participate in meetings, activities, or community opportunities.
  • Failure to use the membership or its available benefits.
  • Personal scheduling conflicts or lack of availability.
  • Dissatisfaction arising solely from a member’s expectations of business opportunities, leads, referrals, or commercial outcomes, which cannot be guaranteed.

However, genuine and exceptional circumstances may be considered for a refund at our discretion, subject to the facts of the case and applicable law.

Nothing in this Policy excludes any refund, cancellation, or other right that cannot legally be waived.

4 Genuine Refund Requests

We understand that exceptional circumstances may arise. Refund requests may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis where there is a genuine reason, including:

  • Duplicate payment for the same membership.
  • An incorrect or unauthorised charge that is verified through the relevant payment process.
  • A technical or payment-processing error resulting in an amount being charged incorrectly.
  • A material failure to provide the purchased membership that is attributable to us and cannot reasonably be resolved.
  • Other exceptional circumstances that we consider reasonable and appropriate.

The examples above are illustrative and do not guarantee approval. Each request will be assessed on its individual merits and in accordance with applicable law.

5 How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, email connect.f4f@gmail.com with the subject line “Refund Request – Founder for Founder.”

Required Information to Include:

  • Your full name and registered email address.
  • The payment date and amount.
  • Your transaction or payment reference, if available.
  • The reason for your request.
  • Any supporting information relevant to your case.

We may request additional information where reasonably necessary to verify the payment and assess the request.

We will review the request and communicate our decision within a reasonable period. If a refund is approved, it will ordinarily be processed through the original payment method where supported by the payment provider. The time taken for the funds to appear may depend on the payment provider or bank.

6 Membership Cancellation

Members may request cancellation of their recurring membership subscription using the cancellation facility provided on the Platform, where available, or by contacting connect.f4f@gmail.com.

Cancellation will stop future subscription renewals in accordance with the subscription terms and the payment provider’s processing requirements. Members should submit cancellation requests before the next renewal is processed.

Unless otherwise required by law or expressly stated in the applicable membership terms:

  • Cancellation does not automatically entitle a member to a refund of a payment already processed.
  • Membership access may continue until the end of the paid billing period, unless the membership is suspended or terminated earlier under the applicable terms.
  • Cancellation of a recurring subscription does not automatically delete the member’s account or personal information. Account deletion may be requested separately, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

If a renewal payment is processed after a timely cancellation request because of a verified processing error, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

7 Membership Suspension or Termination

Founder for Founder may suspend or terminate a membership in accordance with its Terms & Conditions, including in cases of serious misconduct, misuse of the Platform, or violation of applicable rules.

Suspension or termination due to a member’s conduct or breach of the Terms & Conditions will not ordinarily entitle the member to a refund for the unused portion of a billing period, except where required by applicable law or determined otherwise by us.

Where a membership is discontinued by us for reasons unrelated to a member’s conduct, any refund or adjustment will be assessed in accordance with the circumstances, the applicable membership terms, and applicable law.

8 Duplicate Payments and Payment Errors

If you believe you have been charged twice, charged an incorrect amount, or charged due to a payment-processing error, please contact us promptly with the relevant transaction details.

After verification, any confirmed duplicate payment or incorrect charge will be addressed appropriately, including a refund where applicable.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our membership model, payment processes, operations, or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be published on the Platform with its revised “Last Updated” date.

Any changes will apply in accordance with applicable law and will not remove rights that have already accrued to members.
