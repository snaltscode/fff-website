Core Tenet

Every boardroom confession, cap table dispute, or internal distress shared within f4f remains strictly governed by the Chatham House Rule. Information disclosed in roundtables or closed circles may never be attributed directly or indirectly outside the circle.

check_circle Zero Hard-Selling: Direct pitch desks, unsolicited product sales, or pitching advisory retainers to fellow founders without explicit request is strictly barred.
check_circle Mutual Discretion: Taking screenshots of intra-app threads, sharing member contact info with recruiters or agencies triggers automatic termination.

The f4f bulletin board and intra-founder channels exist exclusively for mission-critical inquiries: vendor recommendations, key executive backchanneling, international regulatory introductions, and institutional banking guidance.

Prohibited Activity

Multi-level marketing, crypto token shills, generic lead magnets, or automated broadcast requests result in immediate removal of network privileges.

Founder Dinners

Strict RSVP commitment. Cancellations inside 24 hours require seat reimbursement and ombudsman review.

Sports & Tracks

Padel, tennis, and run-clubs emphasize athletic collegiality over corporate hierarchy.

check_circle Active Presence: Devices on silent during intimate salons. Prioritize dialogue over digital distraction.

When cross-referring clients, co-investing via syndicates, or entering vendor partnerships with fellow members, transparency is paramount.

handshake Double Opt-In Intros: Never introduce a peer to an external investor or candidate without prior written consent.
price_check Finder Fees Disclosure: Any economic interest or referral compensation between members must be declared upfront.
gavel Non-Negotiable Threshold

Breach of NDA, leakage of member data to third-party press, harassment, deceptive financial disclosures, or predatory talent poaching will result in permanent expulsion and reciprocal revocation across partner networks.

If you encounter friction, unaligned conduct, or integrity issues with another founder, lodge an expedited case with our peer arbitration council.
