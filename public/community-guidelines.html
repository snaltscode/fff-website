1 Our Community Philosophy

Founder for Founder is a curated business network built for founders, entrepreneurs, and professionals who value meaningful relationships, mutual respect, collaboration, and long-term growth.

Our community is designed to encourage productive conversations, relevant business connections, credible opportunities, and shared learning.

Every member contributes to the quality of this network. Membership is a privilege accompanied by the responsibility to maintain a respectful, professional, and constructive environment.

2 Respect Every Member

Treat every member with courtesy and professionalism, regardless of their business size, experience, background, designation, or professional standing.

Members must not engage in:

  • Personal attacks, insults, abusive language, harassment, or intimidation.
  • Public shaming, targeted humiliation, or attempts to provoke other members.
  • Discrimination or hateful conduct.
  • Repeated arguments intended to disrupt discussions rather than resolve issues.
  • Threats, bullying, or retaliation against members who disagree or raise genuine concerns.

Golden Rule: Disagreement is acceptable. Disrespect is not.

3 Zero Tolerance for Spam

The community is not a platform for indiscriminate advertising or unsolicited solicitation.

The following activities are strictly prohibited unless expressly permitted through an official community feature or authorised by the administration:

  • Repeated promotional posts, advertisements, or sales pitches.
  • Posting the same message, offer, link, or request multiple times.
  • Unsolicited bulk messages, cold pitches, or repetitive direct messages to members.
  • Irrelevant links, unrelated content, chain messages, or engagement bait.
  • Repeatedly tagging members or administrators to attract attention.
  • Using the community primarily to collect contact details, generate unrelated traffic, or solicit sales.
  • Creating multiple accounts to promote the same business or bypass moderation.

Designated Channels: Promotional content must be relevant, appropriate, and shared exclusively through designated opportunity features or channels.

4 Meaningful Use of Needs & Wants

The Needs & Wants feature exists to help members communicate genuine business requirements and relevant opportunities.

Members should:

  • Describe their requirements clearly and honestly.
  • Include relevant details, such as the type of service, product, expertise, or connection required.
  • Share opportunities that are relevant to the network.
  • Avoid vague, misleading, exaggerated, or repeatedly reposted requests.
  • Update or close a request when it has been fulfilled or is no longer relevant.
  • Respect the other party’s time, budget, confidentiality, and decision-making process.

Do not post fake requirements, fabricated opportunities, misleading offers, or requests designed solely to promote your own business.

5 Leads, Referrals, and Business Conduct

Business introductions and referrals should be based on relevance, transparency, and mutual consent.

Members must not:

  • Fabricate leads, referrals, client relationships, testimonials, or business credentials.
  • Misrepresent their products, services, expertise, pricing, or ability to deliver.
  • Claim a referral or introduction without a reasonable basis.
  • Pressure other members into purchasing, investing, partnering, or making introductions.
  • Share another member’s confidential information without permission.
  • Misuse an introduction to repeatedly solicit unrelated products or services.

Members are encouraged to communicate expectations clearly and honour commitments made to other members.

Note on Facilitation: Founder for Founder facilitates connections but does not guarantee the quality, accuracy, performance, or commercial outcome of every member or referral.

6 No Unnecessary Drama or Disruptive Behaviour

We encourage members to resolve disagreements constructively and keep community discussions focused on their intended purpose.

The following behaviours may result in moderation:

  • Repeatedly derailing discussions with unrelated complaints or personal disputes.
  • Turning professional disagreements into prolonged public arguments.
  • Repeatedly posting provocative messages to attract attention or create conflict.
  • Making baseless allegations, spreading rumours, or deliberately misrepresenting another member’s statements.
  • Flooding discussions with complaints after the administration has acknowledged the matter and provided a reasonable response.
  • Organising other members to target, intimidate, or disrupt an individual or the community.
  • Refusing to follow reasonable moderation instructions and repeatedly restarting a closed dispute.

Raising a genuine concern is always permitted. Members may contact the administration privately to report misconduct, unfair treatment, suspected fraud, or a moderation issue. Good-faith complaints will not be treated as misconduct simply because they are critical.

The administration may move sensitive disputes to a private channel to protect members’ privacy and minimise disruption.

7 Professional Communication

Communicate with clarity, courtesy, and purpose.

Members should avoid excessive tagging, repeated follow-ups, aggressive demands for immediate responses, and messages that create unnecessary pressure.

Respect that members have different schedules and responsibilities. No member is obligated to respond to every message, accept every invitation, purchase a service, or enter a business relationship.

Boundary Respect: Members must respect a clear refusal and must not continue pursuing another member after being asked to stop.

8 Privacy and Confidentiality

Members must respect the privacy of others and the confidentiality of community interactions.

Without appropriate permission, members must not:

  • Publish screenshots of private conversations or member-only discussions.
  • Share personal phone numbers, email addresses, financial information, or other non-public details.
  • Distribute confidential business information, proposals, or documents.
  • Record or redistribute private meetings or events where doing so is not authorised.
  • Use member information for unrelated marketing, bulk outreach, or external databases.

These restrictions do not prevent lawful reporting of misconduct or sharing information where legally required.

9 Authenticity and Accountability

Members are expected to provide accurate information about their identity, professional background, business, and offerings.

Impersonation, fraudulent activity, fake profiles, misleading claims, manipulated testimonials, and deliberate misrepresentation are strictly prohibited.

If a member identifies an error in a post or claim, they should correct it promptly. Honest mistakes may be addressed constructively; deliberate or repeated deception may result in disciplinary action.

10 Events and Meetings

Members participating in Founder for Founder events, meetings, activities, or challenges must:

  • Respect the event schedule, venue, organisers, speakers, and fellow attendees.
  • Follow registration, attendance, safety, and venue requirements.
  • Avoid aggressive selling, disruptive interruptions, or monopolising group discussions.
  • Seek consent before recording, photographing, or publicly sharing identifiable images or private conversations where appropriate.
  • Honour confirmed commitments or notify the organisers promptly if unable to attend.
  • Behave professionally during introductions, networking, and follow-up interactions.

The administration may restrict participation in future events when conduct repeatedly disrupts the experience of others.

11 Fair Use of Community Features

Members must use the Platform, directory, messaging features, and other community tools for their intended professional purposes.

Attempts to bypass access restrictions, scrape member information, manipulate engagement, misuse reporting features, or interfere with the operation of the Platform are prohibited.

Access to member information does not grant permission to use it for unrelated commercial purposes.

12 Moderation and Consequences

Founder for Founder reserves the right to moderate content and take proportionate action when these guidelines or the Platform’s Terms & Conditions are violated.

Depending on the nature, severity, frequency, and impact of the conduct, actions may include:

  • Informal reminder: For minor or first-time issues.
  • Formal warning: For repeated or more serious violations.
  • Content removal or feature restriction: Where content or behaviour is inappropriate or disruptive.
  • Temporary suspension: Where further review or a cooling-off period is necessary.
  • Membership termination: For serious misconduct, fraud, harassment, repeated spam, retaliation, or continued violations after warnings.
  • Immediate protective action: Where necessary to address credible threats, suspected fraud, serious harassment, or risks to member safety.

The administration may skip earlier steps when the seriousness of the situation warrants it. Where reasonably appropriate, members may be given an opportunity to explain their position before a final decision.

Membership fees and refunds following suspension or termination will be governed by the applicable membership terms and Refund and Cancellation Policy, subject to applicable law.

13 Reporting a Concern

If you encounter spam, harassment, misleading business conduct, privacy violations, or other behaviour that breaches these guidelines, report it to the administration at connect.f4f@gmail.com.

Please provide relevant details and supporting evidence where available. Reports will be reviewed as reasonably practicable and handled with appropriate discretion.

Integrity in Reporting: Do not use reporting tools to threaten, harass, retaliate against, or deliberately target another member.

14 Administration and Interpretation

Founder for Founder may interpret and update these guidelines as the community evolves. The administration will seek to apply them consistently, fairly, and in a manner that protects the community’s purpose and its members.

Repeated or serious violations may affect a member’s continued participation in the network.

These Community Guidelines should be read alongside the Platform’s Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Refund and Cancellation Policy. Where a conflict arises, applicable law and the relevant contractual terms will govern.

15 Our Shared Standard

Founder for Founder works best when members bring genuine intent, professional integrity, mutual respect, and a willingness to contribute.

We welcome constructive criticism, different perspectives, honest questions, and ambitious ideas. We do not welcome spam, manipulation, harassment, or behaviour that consistently takes away from the value of the network.

Bring value. Respect boundaries. Build meaningful relationships.

By participating in Founder for Founder, members agree to follow these Community Guidelines.
