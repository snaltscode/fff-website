Article 01 Syndicate Foundations

1. The Core Syndicate Ethos (Create. Connect. Collaborate.)

FOUNDER FOR FOUNDER (F4F) was conceived under an unyielding conviction: peer leadership flourishes only when stripped of vanity metrics, hollow performativity, and transactional sales friction. Our charter unites proven operators, visionary technology architects, and venture-backed founders in an atmosphere of unvarnished candor.

Members do not attend to extract; they convene to contribute. Every exchange must advance one of our three foundational tenets:

handyman Create

Foster original commercial, technological, and intellectual breakthroughs that shift entire markets.

hub Connect

Open vetted pathways to sovereign funds, tier-1 customers, and rare specialized talent with total integrity.

groups_3 Collaborate

Solve existential operational bottlenecks through confidential, vulnerability-tolerant peer counsel.

Article 02 Zero Spam Safeguards

2. Strict Zero-Tolerance Spam & Cold-Pitching Rule

The directory, communications channels, private assemblies, and Needs & Wants database are not sales funnels. Any member who initiates unprompted B2B solicitations, cold calendar links, template outbound scripts, or automated messaging workflows will be expelled permanently.

block

Immediate Termination Triggers:

  • Adding member email addresses or phone numbers to external CRM systems, newsletters, or marketing automations.
  • Direct messaging members on WhatsApp or Telegram pitching agency services without prior bilateral agreement.
  • Circulating unsolicited investment pitch decks or convertible notes outside designated Demo Day chambers.
Article 03 Confidentiality Mandate

3. Chatham House Rule & Absolute Confidentiality

All private assemblies, dinners, and masterminds adhere to the standard Chatham House Rule: participants are free to use the information received, but neither the identity nor the affiliation of the speaker(s), nor that of any other participant, may be revealed.

lock Financial Data & Runway

Shared Burn rates, ARR disclosures, down-round negotiations, and cap tables shared in syndicate chambers are legally bounded by our non-disclosure terms.

no_photography Zero Unauthorized Recording

Audio recordings, screenshotting member rosters, transcriptions, and generative AI note-taker bots are strictly forbidden in all executive sessions.

Article 05 Referral Etiquette

5. Leads & Referrals Consent Protocol

When a fellow member makes a targeted introduction, you are acting as an ambassador of the entire Founder for Founder collective. The Double Opt-In Protocol is mandatory:

Step A Secure Mutual Permission: Never loop a founder into an email chain or messaging group without private, explicit confirmation from both parties first.
Step B Provide a Forwardable Blurb: The requester must supply a concise, two-paragraph self-contained context piece so the connector expends zero drafting effort.
Step C Close the Feedback Loop: Within 48 hours of connection completion, advise the introducer of outcomes and extend professional gratitude.
Article 06 Gathering Protocol

6. Curated Gathering & 1-to-1 Meeting Etiquette

In-person salons, private executive dinners, and synchronized 1-to-1 deep dives represent our highest concentration of trust capital. Members agree to arrive prepared, attentive, and fully present.

Device Minimization

Phones remain silenced and out of sight during table conversations. Urgent escalations are taken discreetly outside the room.

Equal Voice Dynamic

Regardless of funding stage or public acclaim, every seat at the round table carries identical weight. No monopolization of debate.

Article 08 Dignity & Respect

8. Harassment, Discrimination & Code of Conduct

We maintain an unequivocal zero-tolerance stance against harassment, intimidation, derogatory speech, or microaggressions rooted in race, ethnicity, nationality, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, neurodiversity, or religious conviction.

Professional disagreement, rigorous debate, and robust critical thinking are championed; personal humiliation or patronizing conduct is not tolerated under any circumstance.

Article 09 Talent Stewardship

9. Non-Poaching & Talent Etiquette

Founders regularly share team architecture hurdles, org chart restructuring plans, and employee retention strategies in syndicate sessions. Leveraging internal member visibility to secretly poach engineers, department heads, or key staff from an active member company is considered an egregious breach of ethics.

Voluntary Inbound Candidates

If an employee of a member firm applies independently to your firm via public channels, standard hiring discretion applies. However, soliciting through confidential syndicate roster data is grounds for immediate exclusion.

Article 10 Asset Preservation

10. Intellectual Property Respect

Unpatented architectures, private algorithmic methodologies, proprietary go-to-market strategies, and product roadmap teardowns presented inside F4F events remain the sole intellectual property of the presenting venture.

Replicating, reverse-engineering, or commercializing technical assets discovered in internal sessions constitutes actionable copyright and contractual infringement under snalts.co platform agreements.

Article 11 Governance Transparency

11. Conflict of Interest Disclosure

When advising peers, recommending vendors, or facilitating investor introductions where you maintain personal equity stakes, advisory options, affiliate kickbacks, or family relationships, you must declare this conflict openly before offering recommendations.

Article 12 Digital Channels

12. Founders Collective Chat Room Standards

Our private chat groups (Slack, WhatsApp Executive Chambers, and Signal Threads) are curated sanctuaries for operational focus.

Signal-to-Noise Discipline

Avoid chain messages, speculative political commentary, unverified crypto hype, and generic business memes.

Thread Decorum

Utilize organized discussion threads for complex queries to keep root channels clean and immediately scannable.

Article 14 Enforcement Actions

14. Disciplinary Process & Revocation of Membership

Founder for Founder adheres to a swift, multi-tier compliance ladder. The Syndicate Committee reserves sole discretion to suspend or terminate membership at any juncture to preserve network safety:

1
Formal Executive Caution

Issued for minor non-compliance (e.g., late cancellations or peripheral message noise). Recorded on platform ledger.

2
Temporary Chamber Suspension (90 Days)

Access to active assemblies, physical gatherings, and the Needs & Wants database revoked.

3
Permanent Expulsion & Registry Blacklist

Triggered immediately for cold-pitching, confidentiality leaks, or harassment. No refunds provided.
