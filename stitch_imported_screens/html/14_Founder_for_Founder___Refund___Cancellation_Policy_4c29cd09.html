Clause 1.0 Subscription Governance

Membership Subscription Billing & Auto-Renewal

Membership in the Founder for Founder collective is operated as a continuous, recurring tier to ensure sustained access to private syndicate deal flow, verified peer roundtables, and advisory networks. Standard active founder dues are pegged at INR 1,500 per month, exclusive of statutory 18% Goods & Services Tax (totaling INR 1,770 per cycle).

By authorizing an e-mandate via recurring debit, credit card, or UPI autopay, you consent to automated debit on your monthly renewal date. In accordance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) standing instructions, pre-debit notifications are dispatched via SMS and registered email at least 24 hours prior to transaction processing.

Clause 2.0 Voluntary Termination

Voluntary Cancellation & Notice Period

Members retain the unqualified privilege to terminate their recurring membership at any point. To guarantee that your bank does not trigger an unrevoked clearing mandate, cancellations should ideally be processed at least 24 hours prior to your scheduled renewal date.

Upon successful cancellation of auto-renewal:

  • check_circle Uninterrupted Access: Your ecosystem privileges, directory listings, internal Slack/Discord channels, and advisory schedules remain fully operational through the remainder of the currently paid billing period.
  • check_circle No Pro-Rata Cash Refunds: Unutilized calendar days within an ongoing monthly cycle are strictly non-refundable once the cycle has commenced.
  • check_circle Re-Admissions Clause: Re-enrollment after deliberate cancellation is subject to standard queue reviews and prevailing membership dues at the time of re-application.

Clause 4.0 Protection Clauses

Exceptional Refund Scenarios & Application Rejection

We firmly uphold high moral and executive integrity in every financial transaction. While standard subscriptions operate under finite non-refundability, absolute exceptions are honored in the following circumstances:

Admissions Review Fee Rejection Protocol 100% Guaranteed

If an prospective applicant tenders an evaluation or intake pre-authorization fee during admissions and our Admissions Committee determines that the applicant does not meet the collective’s current stage, revenue, or cohort diversity criteria, the fee is refunded in full automatically within 24 hours of the written rejection dispatch.

Technical Duplicate Billing Automated Reversal

In cases of telecommunication failure, network timeouts, or payment gateway double charges resulting in two duplicate deductions within a single 30-day billing cycle, the redundant charge will be refunded instantaneously upon ledger reconciliation.

Clause 6.0 Disputes & Integrity

Chargebacks, Disputes & Failed Payments

In our executive ecosystem, honest dialogue precedes bureaucratic conflict. We request members to consult our operations team prior to filing an adversarial card chargeback through their credit provider. Unsubstantiated chargebacks will prompt immediate suspension of all ecosystem permissions pending formal reconciliation.

When a recurring monthly auto-debit fails owing to expired instruments or limit caps, a grace timeline of 72 hours is observed. Our portal will re-attempt mandate collection twice before gracefully shifting the member profile into an inactive archival state.

Clause 7.0 Disbursement Operations

Refund Processing Timeline (5–7 Business Days)

Once an exceptional refund scenario or admissions rejection refund is stamped as approved by the compliance team, the reimbursement is authorized instantaneously in our payment backend.

Depending on the originating banking partner and nodal clearing infrastructure (National Payments Corporation of India / RBI RTGS windows), funds reflect on statement balances within 5 to 7 operational banking days. Refunds can only be routed back to the explicit funding instrument used in the original payment order; cash or alternate recipient dispersals are strictly banned by anti-money laundering (AML) statutes.
