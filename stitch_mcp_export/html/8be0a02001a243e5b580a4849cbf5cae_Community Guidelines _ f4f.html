1. Chatham House Rule

Confidential

All dialogues, operational metrics, and vulnerabilities shared during closed-door salons remain strictly confidential. What is disclosed within f4f stays within f4f.

2. Zero-Tolerance Spam & Cold Pitching

Zero Tolerance

The network is built for mutual leverage and collaborative growth, not aggressive sales pitching or unsolicited mass messaging. Direct outreach must be context-driven and consented.

3. Authentic Needs & Offers

Synergy

Ensure requirements posted on the Needs & Wants engine represent genuine commercial demands and deliverable solutions. No phantom asks or speculative brokering.

4. Respectful Punctuality & RSVP

Honor Code

Respect fellow founders' limited time. Confirm attendance 24 hours prior; no-shows without emergency notice permanently affect your salon standing and priority invites.

5. Reciprocity & Peer Generosity

Ecosystem

Give before you ask. Share hard-won lessons, provide candid references, and proactively introduce peers to genuine opportunities without expectation of immediate fee cuts.
