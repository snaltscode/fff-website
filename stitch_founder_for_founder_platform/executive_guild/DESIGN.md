## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the quiet authority, institutional gravitas, and high-velocity clarity required for an elite founder network. The aesthetic marries architectural modernism with private-members-club luxury. It avoids startup playfulness, neon tech cliches, and decorative clutter, favoring instead immaculate proportions, structured grid foundations, precision frosted surfaces, and restrained gilded accents.

### Visual Pillars
- **Curated Precision:** Generous negative space paired with razor-sharp editorial alignments.
- **Architectural Depth:** Layered frosted glass panels over crisp chalk-white canvas backgrounds, reinforced with deep evergreen contrast.
- **Understated Prestige:** Warm golden micro-accents deployed strictly for verified credentials, deal milestones, and elite tiers—never for primary navigation or mass UI noise.
- **Absolute Typography Discipline:** High-contrast editorial display serifs paired with a modern geometric grotesque. All text renders strictly upright in normal font-style; italics are banned across all levels to retain structural firmness.

## Typography

The type system blends authoritative editorial heritage with contemporary geometric legibility.

### Rules & Execution
- **Strictly Normal Style:** Under no circumstances should `font-style: italic` or `font-style: oblique` be rendered anywhere in this system. Emphasis must be accomplished through weight transitions (Regular to SemiBold/Bold), tonal shift, or subtle container treatment.
- **Headlines:** Set in `Domine` with high contrast and tight tracking. Used for major platform value propositions, founder portfolio spotlights, and institutional tier headers.
- **Body & Controls:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Unrivaled legibility across deal terms, founder bios, metric dashboards, and platform dialogue.
- **Label Status:** Displayed with uppercase casing and expanded tracking (`0.08em`) to enforce structural, credentialized status signifiers across badges and tokens.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is constructed on a 12-column adaptive fluid grid with strict mathematical cadence.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns, 32px (`gutter-lg`) gutters, 48px (`margin-desktop`) outer margins. Max canvas container pinned to 1440px to retain executive editorial balance.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 24px (`gutter`) gutters, 32px (`margin`) outer margins. Two-column founder and syndicate cards scale proportionally.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`gutter-sm`) gutters, 16px (`margin-mobile`) outer margins. Content collapses into single-column vertical flows with full-bleed cards.

### Spacing Philosophy
Internal component padding relies strictly on an 8pt base grid with an intentional 4pt sub-increment (`space-xs`). Component density should remain airy yet mathematically exact, conveying high-value deliberation.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is articulated through structural glassmorphism and soft ambient tinted shadowing rather than heavy dark drop shadows.

### Glassmorphism System
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F8FAF9`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Glass):** `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px); border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8);`
  - Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Founders / Hover / Modal Glass):** `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92); backdrop-filter: blur(24px); border: 1px solid rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.12);`
  - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Executive Overlays & Drawers):** `background: #FFFFFF; border-left: 1px solid #E2E8F0;`
  - Shadow: `-16px 0 48px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08)`.

### Micro-Transitions
All elevation adjustments are paired with high-performance CSS transforms: `transition: transform 200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), box-shadow 200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. On hover, elevated glass cards shift `translateY(-2px)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances architectural crispness with human ergonomics.

### Strict Specifications
- **Button Radius:** Strictly enforced `6px` (`roundedness: 1` modified with bespoke component token) across all buttons, input shells, dropdown triggers, and interactive elements. Pill buttons and heavily circular interactive controls are strictly barred to maintain executive discipline.
- **Card Radius:** Standard cards, glass modals, and executive badges utilize `10px` to `12px` border radius, delivering a clean modern contour without feeling playful.
- **Avatars & Credential Seals:** Founder headshots maintain standard squircle geometry (`8px` to `12px` border radius) or classic round profiles only when embedded into verified badge clusters.
