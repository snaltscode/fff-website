## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural and editorial visual language tailored for high-conviction founders, builders, and operators across India. Eschewing the hyper-gloss and synthetic tropes of generic networking platforms, the visual narrative is rooted in quiet authority, institutional longevity, and human warmth. The aesthetic draws from high-end broadsheet publishing, archival typography, and contemporary minimalist interface design.

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Curated & Discerning:** Interfaces feel deliberate and composed. White space functions as a structural element rather than empty margin.
- **Understated Gravitas:** Authority is communicated through rigorous typographic scale, precise alignment, and disciplined color use, avoiding loud gradients, neon accents, or marketing buzzwords ("exclusive", "elite", "1%").
- **Physicality & Tactility:** Surfaces mirror archival paper stocks (`#faf9f6` canvas), balanced by razor-sharp hairline dividers and ink-dense typographic hierarchy.
- **Unified Cohort Architecture:** A single continuous design vernacular unites the public web presence, multi-step admissions pipeline, authenticated member directories, and operational tooling.

### Visual Movement
The system balances **Editorial Minimalism** with **Institutional Modernism**. High-contrast serif headlines evoke historical journal mastheads, while disciplined sans-serif data layers ensure fast, unambiguous scanning across dense directories, deal memos, and operator profiles.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an unyielding natural light mode. Deep forest emeralds provide focused intentionality, offset by a calibrated parchment canvas and hairline metallic gold details reserved exclusively for verified insignias, peer crests, and archival status marks.

### Palette Architecture & Semantic Mapping
- **Canvas Base (`#faf9f6`):** Natural warm alabaster/newsprint base, preventing eye fatigue while reinforcing editorial tactility.
- **Surface Pure (`#ffffff`):** Reserved for elevated cards, active input modules, and foreground panels against the canvas base.
- **Primary Ink (`#064e3b` / Deep Forest Emerald):** The foundational interactive color. Applied with restraint to primary calls-to-action, key nav links, and focused brand signifiers.
- **Deep Emerald Shade (`#043e2e`):** Used for hover and active button states, deep navigation headers, and focused selection borders.
- **Refined Gold (`#b48608` / `#d4af37`):** Restricted entirely to secondary badges, cohort year insignias, and credential borders. Never used for large text blocks, button backgrounds, or general UI chrome.
- **Neutral Stack:**
  - `Text Primary (#1a1a19)`: High-density carbon for all titles, primary metrics, and body copy.
  - `Text Secondary (#57564f)`: Warm charcoal for metadata, subtitles, and form labels.
  - `Text Tertiary / Muted (#85837a)`: Captions, helper text, and inactive placeholders.
  - `Border Subtle (#e7e5de)`: Hairline 1px structural grid dividers and card strokes.
  - `Border Strong (#c5c2b8)`: Form input perimeters, active segmented control borders.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - `Success`: `#1b5e20` (Forest Green)
  - `Warning`: `#b45309` (Warm Amber)
  - `Critical`: `#991b1b` (Muted Carmine)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the editorial authority of **Domine** with the utilitarian precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

### Typographic Implementation Rules
- **Editorial Headlines (Domine):** Used for narrative signposts, section titles, hero statements, and member quotes. Titles must always use proportional optical kerning and tight leading to maintain typographic density. Never set serif titles in all-caps.
- **Functional Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Standardized across profile descriptions, application prompts, governance rules, and system communications. Line-height is intentionally open (`1.6` ratio) to ensure effortless reading across lengthy bios and long-form operator dispatches.
- **Micro-Copy & Metadata (Plus Jakarta Sans Uppercase):** All category markers, badges, form headers, and breadcrumbs utilize `label-sm` set in clean uppercase styling with `0.06em` positive letter spacing for optimal legibility at micro scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine uses a structured baseline grid with disciplined horizontal containment to project calm, deliberate order.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width containment container locked at `1280px` for portal interfaces and `1140px` for narrative publishing. Standard 12-column layout with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `3rem` (`48px`) margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Multi-column cards compress to 2-column groups.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins. Complex analytical dashboards downscale to vertically stacked, segmented list views.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Section separations strictly follow the exponential curve (`space-2xl` to `space-4xl`). Component interiors conform strictly to the standard scale (`space-xs` through `space-lg`), eliminating arbitrary margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, floating blurs, or skeuomorphic bevels. Visual hierarchy is established via **architectural tonal layering** and **tactile hairline containment**.

### Elevation Stack
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#faf9f6`. Foundation for page layouts, root views, and backdrop canvas.
- **Level 1 (Structural Surface):** `#ffffff` flat background paired with a `1px solid #e7e5de` border. Applied to cards, table bodies, input groups, and directory listings.
- **Level 2 (Active Modals & Drawers):** `#ffffff` with a double perimeter: `1px solid #d4d0c5` and an ambient, micro-diffused shadow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 26, 25, 0.06)`).
- **Level 3 (Popovers, Select Drops, Context Menus):** `#ffffff` surface with `1px solid #c5c2b8` outline and crisp boundary shadow (`0 4px 16px -2px rgba(26, 26, 25, 0.08)`).
- **Hairline Rule:** No element relies on drop shadows alone for boundary definition. Every elevated element must carry a deliberate 1px border stroke.

## Shapes

The interface adheres to an uncompromising, uniform corner radius across all elements to enforce clean, architectural discipline.

### Radius Specifications
- **Standard Radius:** All interactive components—buttons, input fields, containers, modals, table segments, and directory cards—are bound to exactly `6px` (`roundedness: 1` calibrated to `0.375rem` / `6px`).
- **Inner Corner Rule:** Inner nested elements carry an absolute radius of `4px` to maintain strict concentricity inside `6px` containers.
- **Exclusion (Avatars & Status Dots):** Standard user founder avatars and status pips are the only elements permitted a 50% circle border-radius (`rounded-full`). Insignia badges remain rectangular with the standard 6px corner radius.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#064e3b`, text `#ffffff`, height `42px`, padding `0 20px`, radius `6px`. Font is `Plus Jakarta Sans` 14px SemiBold. On hover: background `#043e2e`. Focus: 2px offset ring with `#064e3b`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #c5c2b8`, text `#1a1a19`. On hover: background `#faf9f6`, border `#1a1a19`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle:** Transparent background, text `#57564f`. On hover: background `#f0ede6`, text `#1a1a19`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #fca5a5`, text `#991b1b`. On hover: background `#fef2f2`.

### Chips, Tags & Cohort Marks
- **Cohort / Regional Tag:** Background `#f4f2eb`, text `#57564f`, border `1px solid #e7e5de`, radius `6px`, padding `4px 10px`, font `label-sm`.
- **Founder Insignia / Crest Mark:** Hairline border `1px solid #b48608`, background `#fffdfa`, text `#b48608`. Accompanied by a 6px metallic insignia dot.
- **Status Filter Chip (Selectable):** Interactive tag that toggles between base outline and selected state (background `#064e3b`, text `#ffffff`, border `#064e3b`).

### Form Inputs & Application Fields
- **Text Inputs & Textareas:** Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #d4d0c5`, radius `6px`, padding `10px 14px`, text `#1a1a19`. Placeholder `#85837a`.
- **Focused State:** Border `1px solid #064e3b`, box-shadow `0 0 0 1px #064e3b`.
- **Field Labels:** `label-md`, text `#1a1a19`, margin-bottom `6px`. Optional helper text rendered in `body-sm` (`#57564f`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Box size `18px x 18px`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or circular (radio), border `1.5px solid #85837a`, background `#ffffff`.
- Checked state: Background `#064e3b`, border `#064e3b`, crisp white checkmark glyph.

### Directory & Profile Cards
- Structured on `#ffffff` surfaces with a `1px solid #e7e5de` border and `6px` radius.
- Padding: `24px` internal padding throughout.
- Layout: Top header housing the Founder avatar (`48px` circle) accompanied by primary name (`headline-sm`) and company attribution. Followed by a 2-line bio (`body-md`), separated by a subtle 1px divider, terminating in localized tags (e.g., "Bengaluru Cohort", "Fintech / Seed").

### Admissions Pipeline Stepper (Admissions & Onboarding)
- Horizontal step timeline with sequential nodes. Completed steps show deep emerald fill with white checkmark. Current step features an outer ring in `#064e3b` with a solid inner core. Upcoming steps carry a hairline border `#c5c2b8` on canvas.
